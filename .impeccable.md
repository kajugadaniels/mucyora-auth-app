## Design Context

### Users

MUCYORA Auth serves Rwandan citizens completing account registration, secure
login, password recovery, and live identity verification. People may use the
service on phones, tablets, or desktop computers, including mobile devices in
portrait and landscape orientations. Authentication must remain clear and
usable under time pressure and across varied levels of digital familiarity.

### Brand Personality

Professional, secure, and trustworthy. The experience should communicate calm
competence and careful handling of identity data without feeling institutional,
intimidating, or overly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction

A clean, refined, light-first interface with disciplined use of MUCYORA blue,
strong typography, generous but responsive spacing, and purposeful visual
hierarchy. The design should feel distinctly Rwandan through relevant content
and language rather than decorative stereotypes. Avoid gradients, visual
clutter, generic dashboard cards, glass effects, and ornamental animation.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next secure action immediately understandable.
2. Preserve complete functionality from 320px mobile screens through large
   desktops, including landscape mobile and touch-enabled devices.
3. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, maintain visible keyboard focus, and keep interactive
   targets at least 44 by 44 pixels.
4. Use concise, reassuring language and surface backend validation faithfully.
5. Minimize identity-data exposure and never trade security for visual polish.
